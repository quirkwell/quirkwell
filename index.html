Where can I buy Quirkwell shirts?

Quirkwell designs are sold on TeePublic and Redbubble. Each design on this site links to its product page on both. They print to order and ship worldwide, including the US, UK, EU, Canada and Australia.

What products are available?

Most designs come on classic and premium t-shirts, hoodies, crewneck sweatshirts, long sleeves, kids tees, stickers, mugs, tote bags, phone cases and more. Options vary slightly between Redbubble and TeePublic.

Do you have funny Christmas shirts?

Yes. See the funny Christmas shirt gift guide: Tree Rex (a T-rex Christmas tree), Feral for the Holidays (an opossum in Christmas lights), Snow Way (a grumpy snowman in sunglasses), Unidentified Festive Object (a UFO beaming up a tree), a corgi Gift Wrapping Dept design, and office-party designs like Holiday Spirit Still Installing and Here for the Snack Table.

Order early in December. Delivery times depend on the marketplace and your country, so check the delivery estimate at checkout on Redbubble or TeePublic. Stickers and mugs are good last-minute gifts.
