How to choose

Keep it work-safe

Jokes about the season, emails and meetings land with almost everyone. Avoid anything about a specific person's job or habits.

Fit the budget

Stickers and mugs are the usual Secret Santa price range. A t-shirt or sweatshirt works if your group has a higher limit. Check today's price on TeePublic or Redbubble, as both run frequent sales.

Think about who opens it

For the quiet coworker, try Here for the Snack Table or Replying Next Year. For the one who is always cold and grumpy before coffee, try Powered by Cocoa and Spite.
