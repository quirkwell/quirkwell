How to choose

Hoodies and sweatshirts first

Homebodies live in soft layers, so a hoodie or crewneck gets the most use. Mugs pair well for cocoa and tea people.

For people who are always cold

Emotionally Unavailable Until Spring, a bear hibernating in a snowy cave, and Snow Way, a grumpy snowman, suit anyone who complains about winter.

A small treat

Croissant Therapy and Hot Cocoa Club make easy stickers and mugs for food lovers.

Questions people ask

What do you get someone who loves staying home?

A cozy hoodie or mug with a homebody joke, such as Blanket Burrito League, My Couch Called, I Answered or Low Power Mode.

What is a good gift for someone who is always cold?

A hoodie or sweatshirt with Emotionally Unavailable Until Spring or Snow Way, plus a mug for hot drinks.
