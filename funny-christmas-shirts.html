How to choose

Pick the joke, then the person

Office parties suit gentle jokes about the season itself, like a loading bar for holiday spirit or a checklist that skips small talk. Family photos work better with something everyone can wear, like Christmas Cookie Crew or Snow Way. Animal designs such as Tree Rex, the corgi and the opossum are the safest bet for kids and adults alike.

Choose the product

A t-shirt or sweatshirt works for the party itself. Hoodies make a bigger gift. Stickers and mugs are easy stocking stuffers and Secret Santa add-ons, and they usually arrive faster than clothing.

Order early

Every item is printed to order by TeePublic or Redbubble and shipped from their partners. Check the delivery estimate for your country at checkout, and order by early December if it needs to arrive before Christmas.
